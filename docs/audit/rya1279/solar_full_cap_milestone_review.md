## What fixed the numerical bottleneck

The complete quadrature-weighted MALI operator retains all active-row/emitting-level couplings, including radiatively unconnected pairs, and preserves the old-population physical rate action under overlap. Source oxygen action mismatch is 3.769e-14, versus 0.005703 for the original operator. Physical radiative-rate arrays are bit-identical. Complete coupling alone still left temperature-only timing out.

The finite cap already emits according to top-layer populations. Its incident intensity is eta/chi * (1-exp(-chi*L/mu)). The ordinary formal operator assumed fixed external illumination, giving zero incoming top response. We added only the fixed-extinction derivative with respect to emissivity, -expm1(-chi*L/mu)/chi (limit L/mu), to the preconditioner at the top layer. Actual cap intensity, opacity/emissivity and physical rates are unchanged. Independent analytic and finite-difference compiled controls pass for zero and positive cap lengths and thin/thick extinction. In the full O audit, 851 top-layer operator entries change; deeper entries and physical rates remain bit-identical, with the same 3.769e-14 action identity. No tolerance or resource limit changed. This identifies population-dependent boundary feedback as the remaining slow mode in these cases.
