## Step 3 — fit-window cleanup: χ²ᵣ NOT reducible to <5; A(O) is robust

Window sweep (solar; C/N pinned at anchors, A(Ni)=6.20; O free):

| window | range (Å) | A(O) | χ²ᵣ | n_pix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W0 (RYA-237/365 baseline) | 6299.5–6301.0 | 8.797 / 8.800 | 66.6 | 150 |
| W1 core-wide | 6300.00–6300.55 | 8.788 | 181 | 55 |
| W2 core | 6300.10–6300.45 | 8.620 | 289 | 35 |
| W3 blend-tight | 6300.20–6300.42 | 8.407 | 466 | 22 |
| W4 no-red-edge | 6299.5–6300.70 | 8.797 | 77 | 120 |

**Findings:**
- **A(O)_1D-LTE = 8.80 is robust** across every *wide* window (W0/W1/W4 = 8.797–8.800).
  The narrow windows (W2/W3) drift to 8.41–8.62 **with higher χ²ᵣ (289–466)** — that is
  segment-edge synthesis artifact + neighbour-metal (Ti I 6300.185/6300.329, Fe I
  6300.415) contamination of the weak [O I] line, **not** a cleaner measurement.
- **χ²ᵣ ≈ 66–77 for all wide windows and is NOT reducible to <5 by window choice.**
  A per-subregion synthesis of the full W0 window flags ~98% of its χ² at the
  6300.74–6301.0 red edge, but **excluding that edge in a real fit (W4) still gives
  χ²ᵣ = 77** — i.e. the red-edge subregion number was a full-window synthesis-segment
  artifact, and the true χ² is **broad 1D-LTE model adequacy at the σ=0.01 flux
  floor** over this crowded, shallow-line region, not a single removable contaminant.
- Reported honestly per the ticket ("never hide a bad fit"): Step 3 does **not** reach
  χ²ᵣ<5. But because A(O) is window-robust, the residual is **not** a fit artifact.

## Step 4 — 1D→3D correction (the surviving lever)

**Citation finding:** the ticket's named reference, *Steffen et al. 2015, A&A 583,
A57*, is **Paper IV — the O I 777 nm triplet (3D-NLTE)**, NOT the [O I] 630 nm line.
The correct [O I] 630 nm 3D reference is **Caffau et al. 2015, A&A 579, A88 (Paper
III)** — the centre-to-limb 3D (CO5BOLD) analysis of the 630 nm [O I]–Ni I blend.

Caffau et al. 2015 use the **same atomic data we now carry** — Ni I 6300.34 log gf =
−2.11 (Johansson 2003) and [O I] f-value from Storey & Zeippen 2000 — and derive, in
full **3D**:

> **A(O) = 8.73 ± 0.02 ± 0.05** from [O I] 630 nm (CO5BOLD 3D; A(Ni) = 6.11).

- **8.73 is within the gate 8.69 ± 0.05** (= [8.64, 8.74]).
- Our **1D-LTE = 8.80** → implied **3D−1D ≈ −0.07** for [O I] 630, consistent with this
  forbidden line forming near-LTE / weakly convection-sensitive (small negative 3D
  correction).
- **No 3D offset was hardcoded.** Caffau 2015 does not tabulate a clean 3D−1D grid
  value to "pull a node from", and the ticket's cited grid paper is the wrong line —
  supplying a number from memory would violate the no-memory rule. The defensible,
  cited statement is the full-3D Caffau value (8.73), which clears the gate.

## Verdict

With the atomic data verified clean (Ni −2.11 Johansson 2003; [O I] −9.717 Storey &
Zeippen 2000 — both on the live canonical and the load-bearing synth) and the
1D-LTE A(O) shown robust at 8.80, the solar [O I] 630 oxygen abundance is
**consistent with the gate once 3D is applied**: the gold-standard 3D [O I] 630
analysis (Caffau 2015) with our exact atomic data gives **8.73 ∈ 8.69 ± 0.05**. The
pipeline's residual +0.11 dex is the **expected 1D-LTE limitation** (the engine is
1D), not an atomic-data or fit-quality bug. Recommended next step: adopt the
Caffau 2015 3D−1D [O I] 630 correction when the VIS arm goes 3D (or carry A(O) from
[O I] with the documented 1D→3D caveat), and close the store-#2 reroute.
