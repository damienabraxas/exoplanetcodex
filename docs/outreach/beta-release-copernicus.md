## Science Media Outlets

Contact after arXiv is live. Attach the preprint link. Keep the pitch to 3 sentences.

| Outlet | Audience | Angle |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Sky & Telescope | Serious amateur astronomers | Oldest astronomy magazine; covers independent research |
| Astronomy Magazine | General public | Public-facing, open to independent science |
| New Scientist | Broad educated public | Covers exoplanet stories regularly |
| Ars Technica Science | Tech-savvy general audience | Covers open science well; receptive to independent researchers |

### Pitch framework (3 options — pick one per outlet)

1. **Lead with the science:** “We measured the C/O ratio of 55 Cancri A — the star hosting a potential lava ocean world — and here’s what it means for what that planet is made of.”
2. **Lead with the story:** “Astrophysics thesis in 2010. Fifteen years building lasers and AI systems. Back to the original question: can you read the chemistry of a star and know what its planets are made of?”
3. **Lead with open science:** “All data, all code, all methods — public and reproducible. Here’s the first result.”
