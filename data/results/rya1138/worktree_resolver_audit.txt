RYA-1138 item 5 — path resolution that reads ABOVE its own repo root
============================================================================================
  A resolver that climbs past the repo root reads through the WORKTREE'S
  PARENT. That is canonical only while every worktree shares one parent, and
  it forks silently the moment they do not (RYA-1090: 85 artifacts lost;
  RYA-1138: a baseline that could not import ispec and inverted a merge).
