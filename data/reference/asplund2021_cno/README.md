# Asplund, Amarsi & Grevesse 2021 (A&A 653, A141) Table 3 -- atomic C, N, O rows

`asplund2021_table3_atomic.csv`: the [C I], C I, N I, [O I] and O I rows of Table 3 ("C, N, and
O abundances in the solar photosphere as inferred from the available indicators and from
different model atmospheres"), non-LTE and LTE on the 3D, <3D>, MARCS and Holweger & Mueller
(1974) models, transcribed from the local PDF (Reference documents/aa40445-21.pdf, p. 8).
The molecular rows are not copied: molecules take Amarsi+2021's terms (RYA-1317).

Use (RYA-1315): Asplund+2021 Sect. 2.1's atmosphere terms for the species Scott+2015 does not
tabulate -- half (<3D> - HM) and half (3D - <3D>), from the NON-LTE columns (the ones Asplund
adopts for these indicators; for [C I]/[O I] they equal LTE). `forbidden` = 1 marks [C I] 8727
and [O I] 6300/6363, which a product's pool is matched to by wavelength.
