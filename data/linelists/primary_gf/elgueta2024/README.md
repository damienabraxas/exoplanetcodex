# Elgueta et al. 2024, MNRAS 532, 3694 -- Ca rows of Table 8

Transcribed from the arXiv version (arXiv:2307.00158, PDF p. 18, Table 8 "Calibration of
log gf of lines other than Fe I"). Columns per source list (VALD, MB99 = Melendez & Barbuy
1999): original log gf, astrophysically calibrated log gf, and in brackets the standard
deviation of the per-spectrum offsets and their number. Wavelengths are air: every row that
exists in canonical_gf matches its air wavelength to 0.001 A.

The calibration is against Luck (2018) Cepheid abundances, not the Sun, so it is not
circular for a solar measurement. On the Fe I lines of their Table 7 that we hold with a
laboratory gf (9944.206, 10216.313, 10863.518) their calibrated values sit -0.14 dex below
the lab values (sd 0.05): the scale may run low, which would read solar Ca high.
