# RYA-515 — per-line provenance for the live Fe pool

Generated by `scripts/rya515_fe_perline_provenance.py` (re-runnable; `--check` asserts
reproduction). Product store `data/products/solar/Fe.json`, 70 live Fe products.
Regenerated 2026-09-07 against `main` after RYA-1191 merged — see §6, which is the finding
that regeneration produced.

**Validate, don't tune (RYA-161).** No product value is moved by this ticket. Everything
below is measurement and flagging.

## 1. What is reachable, and what is not

| | count |
|---|---|
| live Fe products | 70 |
| per-line evidence resolved | **29** |
| no artifact on disk | 29 |
| artifact on disk but it CONTRADICTS the product (§6) | 12 |
| per-line rows stamped | 1464 |

`data/results/band_products/` is gitignored and only force-added files survive a fresh
checkout, so **per-line provenance is not currently a durable property of a product** — it
exists for whichever artifacts someone remembered to `git add -f`. Sirius holds more,
scattered across worktrees at various commits; those were deliberately NOT used, because
pulling a line table from an uncommitted worktree state is provenance laundering (RYA-772).

**This must become a permanent output.** A product that cannot show its lines cannot be
audited, and 41 of 70 currently cannot — 29 because the evidence is absent, 12 because the
evidence present disagrees with the published number (§6).

**Flag — resolution had to be made strict.** A first-hit glob resolved 50 products but was
silently wrong for 18: the tier and treatment stems nest (`GRADED` inside `DEEPGRADED`,
`1D-LTE` inside `synth-1D-LTE-gerber`) and the near-UV and VIS artifacts of a holding differ
only in the wavelength stem. Resolution is by exact field decomposition, disambiguated on the
band range, and **gated on `n_lines` value-equality** with the product. `n_lines` counts
`in_aggregate` rows, not rows on disk. The gate is applied to *every* candidate rather than
only to a lone survivor — see §6, where two artifacts of one product coexist on disk and only
value-equality tells them apart.

**Flag — resolution must key on `selector`, not `tier`.** Two live products can agree on
ion, band, holding, tier, route *and* treatment and still be different products. HARPS Fe I
VIS DEEPGRADED 1D-LTE publishes **7.339** under selector `DEEPGRADED-LOCALRENORM` and
**7.535** under `DEEPGRADED` — 0.196 dex apart, the exact trap RYA-1112 recorded. Resolving on
tier stamped the single artifact on disk onto **both**; its median is 7.535, so the
LOCALRENORM product was being shown another product's 109 lines. Keying on selector drops the
double-stamp (1573 → 1464 rows) and correctly reports LOCALRENORM as having no per-line
evidence at all — only `_products.csv` files exist for it. A provenance table that quietly
serves one product's lines for another is worse than an empty one.

## 2. The EW leg is still live — 10 products

All 10 are `PROFILEFIT`, Fe I VIS GRADED, A 7.431–7.498, and **none has reachable per-line
evidence**. The synthesis leg is otherwise as expected (60 SYNTH).

## 3. Fe II: the deep proof

Six of the 10 live Fe II products resolve; the other four are §6 casualties. The census below
reads the per-line artifacts directly (66 Fe II lines, 134 Fe I control lines) so the
diagnostics cover the full pool even where the published *value* is in dispute.

### 3a. reduced chi-square does NOT discriminate — the control refutes it

| | Fe II | Fe I anchor pool |
|---|---|---|
| median red_chi2 | 114.5 | 71.1 |
| lines with red_chi2 > 5 | 66/66 | 132/134 |

The Fe I pool that carries the **adopted** anchor has the same red_chi2 distribution as Fe II.
Reporting Fe II's red_chi2 as evidence of a defect would have been wrong; the Fe I control is
what makes that statement testable, and it fails. red_chi2 is a property of this synthesis
route, not a Fe II symptom. (RYA-1191 reached the same conclusion independently: a
`red_chi2 > 10` guard flags 1262 of 1366 lines.)

### 3b. per-line diagnostics do not predict the error either

Across the Fe II pool, correlation of recovered A with observed depth **-0.30**, with deepest
interloper **-0.36**, with window occupancy **-0.14**. No single-line diagnostic explains
which line goes wrong. A medians-only reading (interloper 0.951 vs 0.394; contamination 64/66
vs 82/134) is a real *pool* difference but does **not** predict A per line.

### 3c. what does separate the pools: curve-of-growth regime

| | Fe II | Fe I anchor pool |
|---|---|---|
| median observed central depth | 0.908 | 0.502 |
| lines deeper than 0.70 | **100%** | **9%** |
| median observed log(EW/λ) | -1.062 | -1.825 |

Depth and EW are measured directly off the Kitt Peak atlas (the synthesis route leaves
`ew_mA`, `rew` and `observed_depth` NaN). The atlas is read as **air** — verified, not
assumed: the deepest minimum near Fe II 4303.170 lands +0.004 Å away read as air and +0.183 Å
read as vacuum — and used at its shipped normalisation (RYA-1026).

**Every line in the live Fe II pool is a strong, saturated line; the Fe I anchor pool almost
entirely avoids that regime.** 0.76 dex of REW separates them. On the flat branch the profile
constrains abundance weakly and the answer is set by damping and microturbulence instead,
which is why the Fe II per-line spread is 2.5–3.0 dex against Fe I's 1.0 and why no per-line
diagnostic correlates. **This is the answer RYA-1081 left open ("saturation UNANSWERED", the
per-line COG being Fe I only, 0 of 9).** It is a pool-selection defect, not a few bad lines.

### 3d. RYA-1081's arm offset reproduces independently — and is now cleaner

Same 8 VIS lines on HARPS and both KP holdings: HARPS − KP is positive on **8 of 8**, median
**+0.288** (vs kpno_molecfit) and **+0.284** (vs kurucz2005). The offset is unchanged whether
4303.170 is in the pool or out of it, and identical across both HARPS stems — so it is an arm
property, not an artifact of the disputed line. The published HARPS 7.966 is that offset
applied to KP's 7.569, pool-wide.

### 3e. Fe II 4303.170 — ruled an artifact, and the ruling is now live

**Fe II 4303.170 Å**, A = 10.011 / 10.010 on the two HARPS products: **+2.5 dex**, and 1.45 dex
beyond the next-worst line in the pool. Its ±0.62 Å fit window holds 65 transitions, **36 of
them CH** — it sits in the G band — with an Nd II interloper at depth 0.637. It is a blend
swallowed by a fit. See `plots/rya515_fe2_fitplots.png`.

RYA-1191 ratified this and wrote it into `data/catalog/line_curation_exclusions.csv`
(ruled 2026-09-07), citing this ticket's transition count. **The ruling is live in code.**
Its consequence for the published products is §6 — and it is larger on ENGINE-A than the
9-line medians suggested, because the ENGINE-A pool is only 3 lines and 4303.170 is one of
the three.

## 4. NLTE correction coverage — flag

`ENGINE-A` is honest: real Bergemann per-line deltas, **332/332 aggregated rows nonzero**,
−0.008..+0.020, with explicit `ENGINE-A-NOT-SERVED` rows (77) where coverage is missing.

**`ENGINE-B-NLTE` and `synth-mean3D-NLTE-gerber-stagger` carry `nlte_delta_dex = 0.0` and
`nlte_source = "none — LTE, no departure applied"` on all 134 aggregated lines each** — while
their treatment axis claims NLTE.

This is a **stamp defect, not a phantom product**. Comparing to the LTE sibling per line:
median NLTE−LTE **+0.037** (kurucz2005, n=67, 0 identical) and **+0.032** (molecfit, n=67,
1 identical), range −0.14..+0.11. The NLTE synthesis genuinely ran; the departures are applied
*inside* the radiative transfer via the Gerber deck rather than as an additive post-hoc delta,
and the per-line schema has no field for that — so it records the correction as absent.

Note the trap this walked into: at product level `solar_kpno_molecfit_corrected` reads
mean3D-LTE and mean3D-NLTE identically to 4 dp, which reads as "nothing ran". The per-line
data refutes it — the medians coincided. Product-level numbers cannot answer a provenance
question; this is the ticket's own thesis, demonstrated against itself.

## 5. A(Fe I) behind the 7.466 anchor

7.466 is a **reference** value, not a pool median: `solar_abundances_v5.csv` records it as
standing on AGSS21's 7.46 (+0.006), with the v4 derivation chain withdrawn (RYA-819/831).
Our per-line pool: n=1336, median **7.490**, 7.347–7.641 (16–84%).

The ticket asks whether this is "a well-behaved distribution, not a mean hiding outliers."
It is well-behaved *as a median* and would not be as a mean. The pool has a real right tail —
skew **+3.73**, with 47 lines above 8.0, 20 above 8.5 and 8 above 9.0 (3.5% / 1.5% / 0.6%),
plus 29 below 7.0. That tail pulls the **mean** to 7.519, **+0.029** off the median. The
median does not move: restricting to the 7.0–8.0 core (n=1260) shifts it by **−0.003**.

So the answer is conditional, and worth stating as such: the aggregation this pool is read
with (median, RYA-1084) is resistant to its own tail; the same pool read as a mean would carry
+0.029 dex of outlier contamination — comparable to the whole published statistical error.
The tail is not hidden, but it is only harmless because of the estimator.
See `plots/rya515_fe1_anchor.png`.

## 6. 🔴 Twelve products contradict their own committed evidence

This is what regenerating the audit against post-RYA-1191 `main` found, and it is the most
consequential result here.

RYA-1191 curated 4303.170 out and re-ran. The **artifacts** moved. The **published products
did not.** Six live Fe II VIS products now disagree with the per-line evidence committed
beside them:

| holding | treatment | published A / n | artifact A / n | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| harps_molecfit | 1D-LTE | 7.966 / 9 | 7.931 / 8 | **−0.035** |
| harps_molecfit | ENGINE-A | 7.617 / 3 | 7.533 / 2 | **−0.084** |
| kpno_kurucz2005 | 1D-LTE | 7.592 / 9 | 7.570 / 8 | **−0.022** |
| kpno_kurucz2005 | ENGINE-A | **7.466** / 3 | 7.326 / 2 | **−0.140** |
| kpno_molecfit | 1D-LTE | 7.569 / 9 | 7.559 / 8 | **−0.010** |
| kpno_molecfit | ENGINE-A | 7.460 / 3 | 7.330 / 2 | **−0.130** |

Two different failure shapes, and the second is the dangerous one:

- **KP (4 products)** — RYA-1191 regenerated the artifact *in place*. The committed evidence
  now has 8 lines; the product still publishes the 9-line number. Product and evidence
  disagree in the same directory, and the `n_lines` gate is what catches it.
- **HARPS (2 products)** — RYA-1191 wrote the re-measurement to a **different wavelength
  stem** (`4200_6908` beside the shipped `4200_6910`; the generator emits 6908, the committed
  product was built at 6910). Both files are on disk. The old one still matches the published
  number exactly, so **nothing looks wrong** — the stale evidence vouches for the stale
  product, and only knowing that a second artifact exists reveals it. These are the only two
  rows in the entire 1464-row provenance table that still aggregate 4303.170.

**Consequence.** All six live Fe II VIS products stand on a line pool that this repo has
already ruled invalid, in a registry that cites this ticket. They are reproducible and they
are not correct — which is precisely the distinction RYA-515 was opened to draw.
**None of the six stands as-is.** The two ENGINE-A KP products are worst: a 3-line median
containing a +2.5 dex artifact, falling to n=2 and moving −0.13/−0.14 once it is removed.

Note `solar_kpno_kurucz2005_corrected` ENGINE-A publishes **7.466** — the Fe I anchor's own
digits, on a different scale, from a 3-line pool whose own evidence says 7.326. Nothing joins
the two; the coincidence is exactly the trap RYA-1112 recorded.

**Eight further products (Fe I) fail the same gate**, all in the two bands RYA-1191 touched,
and the drift has a known cause in every case — this is re-run debt, not an adjudicated
defect:

| band | holding | treatment | published n | artifact n |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| red-optical | kpno_kurucz2005 | 1D-LTE | 45 | 70 |
| red-optical | kpno_kurucz2005 | ENGINE-A | 26 | 35 |
| red-optical | kpno_molecfit | 1D-LTE | 56 | 53 |
| red-optical | kpno_molecfit | ENGINE-A | 28 | 27 |
| NIR | iag | 1D-LTE | 25 | 26 |
| NIR | iag | ENGINE-A | 6 | 7 |
| NIR | kpno_molecfit | 1D-LTE | 26 | 23 |
| NIR | kpno_molecfit | ENGINE-A | 7 | 6 |

Two of the three causes are legible in the artifacts' own `excluded_reason` column:

- The kurucz2005 red-optical artifact carries **70 rows, none excluded** — the pool *grew*
  (45→70) by lines becoming measurable at all, which is RYA-1191 lifting the telluric
  quarantine on that holding once the band was verified corrected.
- The molecfit pools **shrink** because RYA-1191's fit-validity bound removed non-convergent
  fits: red-optical drops 4 of 57 rows (`FIT-NOT-PHYSICAL` ×3 at A = 5.508 / 9.886 / 10.166,
  plus one `NON-MINIMUM`), NIR drops 5 of 28 (`FIT-NOT-PHYSICAL` ×3, `NON-MINIMUM`,
  `edge_pinned`).
- The IAG NIR **+1** is *not* attributable from the artifact alone: its only exclusions are 19
  `ENGINE-A-NOT-SERVED` rows, which do not explain a gain. Left open rather than guessed at.

Every one of these is a line-count change the published product has not been re-derived
through.

**This ticket does not fix any of it** — validate, don't tune. Re-deriving the six Fe II VIS
products against the live curation registry is the follow-up, and it is a value move that
needs Ryan's ruling.
