Product store `data/products/solar/Fe.json` v1.94, 70 live Fe products.

| | count |
|---|---|

| per-line evidence resolved | **30** |
| no artifact on disk | 40 |

Every Fe II product (10/10) resolved, so the Fe II proof below rests on complete evidence.
Fe I resolved 20/60.

`data/results/band_products/` is gitignored and only force-added files survive a fresh
checkout, so **per-line provenance is not currently a durable property of a product** — it
exists for whichever artifacts someone remembered to `git add -f`. Sirius holds ~190 more
scattered across worktrees at various commits; those were deliberately NOT used, because
pulling a line table from an uncommitted worktree state is provenance laundering (RYA-772).

**Flag — resolution had to be made strict.** A first-hit glob resolved 50 products
but was silently wrong for 18: the tier and treatment stems nest (`GRADED` inside
`DEEPGRADED`, `1D-LTE` inside `synth-1D-LTE-gerber`) and the near-UV and VIS artifacts of a
holding differ only in the wavelength stem. Resolution is now by exact field decomposition,
disambiguated on the band range, and **gated on `n_lines` value-equality** with the product.
`n_lines` counts `in_aggregate` rows, not rows on disk.

## 3. Fe II: the deep proof

### 3a. reduced chi-square does NOT discriminate — the control refutes it

| | Fe II | Fe I anchor pool |
|---|---|---|
| median red_chi2 | 114.5 | 85.1 |
| lines with red_chi2 > 5 | 21/21 | 67/67 |

The Fe I pool that carries the **adopted** anchor has the same red_chi2 distribution as Fe II.
Reporting Fe II's red_chi2 68–1212 as evidence of a defect would have been wrong; the Fe I
control is what makes that statement testable, and it fails. red_chi2 is a property of this
synthesis route, not a Fe II symptom.

### 3b. per-line diagnostics do not predict the error either

Across the Fe II pool, correlation of recovered A with observed depth **-0.30**,
with deepest interloper **-0.25**, with window occupancy **-0.04**.
No single-line diagnostic explains which line goes wrong. A medians-only reading (interloper
0.918 vs 0.394) is a real *pool* difference but does **not** predict A per line.

### 3c. what does separate the pools: curve-of-growth regime

| | Fe II | Fe I anchor pool |
|---|---|---|
| median observed central depth | 0.861 | 0.502 |
| lines deeper than 0.70 | **100%** | **9%** |
| median observed log(EW/λ) | -1.151 | -1.825 |

Depth and EW are measured directly off the Kitt Peak atlas (the synthesis route leaves
`ew_mA`, `rew` and `observed_depth` NaN). The atlas is read as **air** — verified, not assumed:
the deepest minimum near Fe II 4303.170 lands +0.004 Å away read as air and +0.183 Å read as
vacuum — and used at its shipped normalisation (RYA-1026).

**Every line in the live Fe II pool is a strong, saturated line; the Fe I anchor pool almost
entirely avoids that regime.** 0.67 dex of REW separates them. On the flat branch the profile
constrains abundance weakly and the answer is set by damping and microturbulence instead,
which is why the Fe II per-line spread is 2.5–3.0 dex against Fe I's 1.0 and why no per-line
diagnostic correlates. **This is the answer RYA-1081 left open ("saturation UNANSWERED", the
per-line COG being Fe I only, 0 of 9).** It is a pool-selection defect, not a few bad lines.

### 3d. RYA-1081's arm offset reproduces independently

Same 9 VIS lines on three holdings: HARPS − kpno_molecfit is positive on **8 of 9**, median
**+0.249 dex**. The published HARPS 7.966 is that offset applied to kpno's 7.569, pool-wide.

### 3e. one line is an unambiguous artifact — flag

**Fe II 4303.170 Å**, A = 10.011 / 10.060 / 10.075 on the three VIS holdings: **+2.5 dex**, and
1.45 dex beyond the next-worst line in the pool. Its ±0.62 Å fit window holds 65 transitions,
**36 of them CH** — it sits in the G band — with an Nd II interloper at depth 0.637. It is a
blend swallowed by a fit. See `results/plots/rya515_fe2_fitplots.png`.

**It does not, however, drive the published value.** Dropping it moves the median by only
−0.035 / −0.022 / −0.011 dex; a 9-line median is resistant. The product does not stand
because of 3c, not because of this line.

**It does drive the ENGINE-A products.** Fe II VIS ENGINE-A aggregates only **3 of 9** lines
(6 are `ENGINE-A-NOT-SERVED`), and 4303.170 is **one of the three**. A 3-line median with a
known +2.5 dex artifact in it does not stand.

## 4. NLTE correction coverage — flag

`ENGINE-A` is honest: real Bergemann per-line deltas, 301/353 nonzero, −0.002..+0.018,
with explicit `NOT-SERVED` rows where coverage is missing.

**`ENGINE-B-NLTE` and `synth-mean3D-NLTE-gerber-stagger` carry `nlte_delta_dex = 0.0` and
`nlte_source = "none — LTE, no departure applied"` on all 134 aggregated lines each** — while
their treatment axis claims NLTE.

This is a **stamp defect, not a phantom product**. Comparing to the LTE sibling per line:
median NLTE−LTE **+0.037** (kurucz2005) and **+0.032** (molecfit), range −0.14..+0.11, with only
1 of 134 rows identical. The NLTE synthesis genuinely ran; the departures are applied *inside*
the radiative transfer via the Gerber deck rather than as an additive post-hoc delta, and the
per-line schema has no field for that — so it records the correction as absent.

Note the trap this walked into: at product level `solar_kpno_molecfit_corrected` reads
mean3D-LTE **7.5520** and mean3D-NLTE **7.5520**, identical to 4 dp, which reads as "nothing
ran". The per-line data refutes it — the medians coincided. Product-level numbers cannot
answer a provenance question; this is the ticket's own thesis, demonstrated against itself.
