# RYA-1173 — the RYA-946 Solar reference-line-set census for Al

Closes **RYA-1141 check D3**: Al was stamped `FROZEN` on 168 manifest rows while this census did
not exist and no exception was recorded.

Everything here is generated by `scripts/rya1173_al_agss21_census.py`; `--check` reproduces it and
refuses on drift. The line set it consumes is `data/reference/asplund2021_al/`.

## Headline

🔴 **Al I 10768.363 A -- 4p 2Po(1/2) -> 5d 2D(3/2), one of the SIX lines carrying AGSS21's adopted A(Al) = 6.43 -- is ABSENT from canonical_gf under any species.**

32 rows of C/Fe/Si/Na/Ti/Cr/Mg/Ca lie between 10700 and 10850 A, and its own multiplet partner is held at 10782.045 A. The line is specifically missing.

A replication of AGSS21's Al on our line list can measure at most 5 of the 6 lines. Codex cannot reproduce that solar value as published until the line is ingested.

🔴 Every log gf in the AGSS21-lineage Al set is Opacity Project / TOPbase LS-coupling THEORY (Mendoza et al. 1995), on all seven lines and in both primaries.

🔴 Al I 8912.900 A: Codex holds log gf = -2.348 (KURUCZ) against the source's -1.963 (TOPbase). A 0.385 dex disagreement on a line the AGSS21 lineage uses -- 3.5x the next largest delta in the set.

✅ The two lines Scott weights highest -- 6696.015, 6698.672 A, weights 2+3 of the 9 weight units spread over the SIX used lines -- are exactly the two Al I lines Codex holds at LAB tier, from Burheim et al. 2023.

⚠️ **This census discharges the RYA-946 gate ONLY. Al is not FROZEN_READY_FOR_MEASUREMENT: RYA-1151, RYA-1174, RYA-1176 and RYA-1177 remain open, and RYA-1173-F1 is new.**
