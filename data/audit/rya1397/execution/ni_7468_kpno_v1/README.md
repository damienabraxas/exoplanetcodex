# Solar N I 7468 Å red-optical diagnostic

Caffau et al. (2009), Table 1, selected this N I feature. The Kitt Peak diagnostic used the existing physical line at 7468.312 Å and its canonical NIST B+ strength. The dedicated fit yielded 7.782 dex in 1D LTE and 7.770 dex with the cited Engine A departure correction. Reduced chi-square was 0.521. The solar table provided no supported 3D NLTE correction for this feature.

Both carbon sensitivity legs (±0.10 dex) finished successfully. Their measured blend contribution is 0.0226 dex. The reassembled budgets are complete for LTE and Engine B (7.782 ± 0.0716 dex) and Engine A (7.770 ± 0.0776 dex). The initial hold and its resolution are recorded in `budget/initial-report.json` and `budget/report.json`.

The same-holding published red-optical LTE pool uses two different indicators and reports 7.942 dex. This single-feature result is 0.160 dex lower, with no supported line-specific 3D correction. Retain the independent fit as a diagnostic pending reconciliation with the existing pool.
