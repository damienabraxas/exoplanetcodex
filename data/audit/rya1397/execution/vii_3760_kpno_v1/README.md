# Solar V II 3760 Å near-UV diagnostic

The Wood et al. (2014) laboratory feature at 3760.2208 Å was matched to the retained GES-style near-UV deck by the lower and upper level and J. The fit uses 3760.217 Å as its center and synthesizes the entire 20-component group. The isolated Kitt Peak fit returned 4.028 dex, with one accepted feature and a formal assembled uncertainty of 0.0587 dex. Engine A had no supported departure correction.

The local pseudo-continuum stayed fixed on both holdings. The molecfit-corrected holding returned reduced chi-square 30.84. The alternate Kurucz 2005 holding returned 4.198 dex, a 0.170 dex shift, with reduced chi-square 26.068 and a profile-fit uncertainty of 0.0654 dex. For context, the existing visible V II Kitt Peak reference pool on the molecfit-corrected holding reports 3.975 dex across five lines.

Both holdings have large profile residuals, and their abundance difference exceeds the quadrature sum of their profile-fit uncertainties. Retain the 3760 Å feature as a diagnostic and preserve the published reference pool. The alternate holding's fit and provenance are in `kurucz2005/`.
