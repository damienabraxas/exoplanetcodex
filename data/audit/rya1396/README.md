## Publication priorities

1. **V I / V II.** The [RYA-733 retrieval](https://linear.app/ryans-adventure-zone/issue/RYA-733) already secured the main experimental sources. Start by transcribing the 11 Biémont-only V II near-UV transitions and matching them by wavelength and energy before suggesting independent grade tests. In K, inspect original Holmes V I rows at 19586.162, 19998.913 and 20230.568 Å and the held match or missing components. The committed V LITSCAN records no usable V I or V II NLTE model and uses an assumed correction in the benchmark lineage. Ma et al. 2022 HFS remained inaccessible in RYA-733 and has alternate held component sources.
2. **Fe, Mg, Si, Ca and Al.** Start with lines already used in Solar products. Confirm original uncertainties where a grade depends on a lifetime scale, a branching fraction or a catalog value. Treat NIST assessed grades and VALD provenance codes as leads to primary sources. A source reflected in multiple catalogs remains one experiment.
3. **Ni, Co, Mn, Sr, Y, Ba and Eu.** The matrix identifies held sources and remaining checks for each. Prioritize the Ni I blend in [O I] 6300, experimental versus calculated Ni II evidence, Co and Mn hyperfine component uncertainties, Sr model/grid applicability, Y set disagreements, Ba isotope mixtures and the Eu II 6645.104 Å source uncertainty. RYA-1268 documents the Eu isotope-adjudicated row as a held decision. Any IR abundance evidence still requires the project’s telluric verification.
4. **Bibliography reconciliation.** The standard `generate_sources_page.py --audit-library` command fails before it scans the folder because current main has 18 invalid `verified` values. The acquisition record supplies the proposed citation key and metadata pending reconciliation with the canonical bibliography. The first pass used independent PDF hashes and local path checks to continue the holdings audit.

This is a document and table triage snapshot. Transition-level supplement verification, per-model applicability and additional access checks remain open. The audit makes no scientific acceptance decisions.
